🔒 Integrity Verification (GPG & Hashes)

We mandate strict verification of all downloaded binaries before execution.

  1. Ensure you have both the `.sha256` hash and `.asc` signature files next to the binary.
  2. Download the official integrity verifier script.
  3. Run it directly against your target binary.
wget https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tilas01/arch-guides-dynamic/main/scripts/verify-integrity.sh
chmod +x verify-integrity.sh
./verify-integrity.sh arch-rusty-security-suite-linux-x86_64
Key Details: The verifier natively pulls `https://github.com/tilas01.keys` to authenticate signatures.
